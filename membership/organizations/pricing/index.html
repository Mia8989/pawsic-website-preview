Organization Partner Membership · Pricing

Your pricing ladder

Base rate $250 per center per year. The per-center rate steps down at every tier as your organization grows. Use the estimator below to see your annual total.

Group One: The Partner Circle

One to fifty centers. Full membership for every site, plus partner recognition. Limited spots for 2026 and 2027.

Group Two: The Enterprise Circle

Fifty-one centers and up. Everything in the Partner Circle, plus onboarding, annual reporting, and measured ROI.

TierCenters or facilitiesPer center / yrDiscountSave per centerAnnual total
Premier Partner 51 to 100 centers $195 22% $55 From $9,945
Elite Partner 101 to 200 centers $185 26% $65 From $18,685
Flagship Partner 201+ centers $175 30% $75 From $35,175

Annual total is centers multiplied by the applicable per-center rate. Each center includes two transferable Premium memberships and unlimited Basic memberships for eligible staff. Rates lock for the contract term; centers added mid-term join at your organization's tier rate. Flexible payment plans available on request. Additional discounts and sponsorships are subject to organizational need and funding availability.

Worked example

150 centers at $27,750 per year

At 150 centers your organization joins the Enterprise Circle as an Elite Partner at $185 per center, versus $37,500 at the base rate. Every center receives two transferable Premium memberships plus unlimited Basic memberships for staff, and your organization earns full PAWSIC Partner recognition, live virtual onboarding with an annual refresher, a standardized annual partnership report, an annual staff satisfaction survey, Wound Basics for your LMS, and an organization-specific PAWSIC landing page.
