Pricing

How pricing works

Annual membership, one rate per center, with tiers that step down as your organization grows.

01

Priced per center, not per person

You pay one annual rate for each center or facility in your organization. Every site receives its two transferable Premium seats and unlimited Basic memberships for staff, however large that staff is.

02

The rate steps down as you grow

Six tiers across two Circles. The more centers you bring, the lower the per-center rate, and the Enterprise Circle adds onboarding, annual reporting, and measured ROI.

03

Payment plans and support are available

Flexible payment plans are available on request. Additional discounts and sponsorships may be available based on organizational need and funding availability, including for individual sites and smaller organizations.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a single center or facility join?

Yes. One center joins on its own as a Founding Partner, with no minimum size and no multi-site commitment. Sponsorship or a needs-based discount may be available. Email Organizations@PAWSIC.org to discuss.

What does transferable mean for the two Premium seats?

Each center designates two Premium members, typically a clinical lead paired with an education, quality, operations, or administrative partner. If one of them leaves the role, the seat moves to their successor at no charge, so turnover never costs you the seat.

Who counts as Basic staff?

Every interdisciplinary clinical and operations staff member at the site, with no cap: CNAs, LPNs, RNs, NPs, DONs, NHAs, dietitians, therapists, social workers, pharmacists, and others.

What happens if we add centers mid-term?

Rates lock for the contract term. Centers added mid-term join at your organization's applicable tier rate for the remainder of the term.

Are payment plans available?

Yes. Flexible payment plans are available on request, and additional discounts and sponsorships may be available based on organizational need and funding availability.

How do we get pricing?

Send us a short inquiry using the form on this page, or email Organizations@PAWSIC.org. We reply with the full pricing ladder for every tier and set up a short call to build a proposal around your organization.
