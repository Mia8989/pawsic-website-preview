The PAWSIC 10 Step Framework to Optimize Skin Health and Pressure Prevention in PAC

Presented by Jeanine Maguire, PhD, MS, MPT, CWS, FCPP, President & Co-Founder, PAWSIC

Watch the course

Choose how you take the course: the full video, the slides only, or audio only. Your place carries over when you switch, and the post-test unlocks when you reach the end.

Audio only58 minutes · follow along with the infographic in Course resources

Chapters

Unlocks when the video ends.

Learning objectives

  1. Identify the 10-Step Framework: reduce PI risk, F-tag citations, infection, and litigation in LTC.
  2. Articulate the regulations and apply the CMS CEP: understand F686 / F684 and map each step to surveyor expectations; use the Critical Element Pathway as a robust internal audit tool.
  3. Evaluate against guidelines and design an action plan: compare your protocols to 2019 / 2025 international standards and implement the 10-Step Framework in your own organization.
