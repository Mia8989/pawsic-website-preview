Events  /  SAWC Fall 2026

SAWC Fall 2026  ·  October 15-18  ·  Las Vegas, NV

Hear PAWSIC Leaders Speak at SAWC Fall

Four PAWSIC board and executive members are on the SAWC Fall 2026 faculty. Three sessions cover mobile wound care compliance, evidence-based practice, and physical therapy in wound healing.

See the Sessions ↓ Register for SAWC Fall →
When
October 15-18, 2026
Where
Las Vegas, NV
PAWSIC sessions
Friday, Oct 16 and Sunday, Oct 18

Three sessions to add to your agenda

All times are Pacific (Las Vegas local time). Two sessions run at the same hour on Friday, so plan with a colleague to cover both.

Friday, October 16

  1. 11:00 AM Ends 12:00 PM Room: Milano 1

    Mobile Wound Management: Compliance and Risk: Staying Legal on the Road

    Developed in collaboration with PAWSIC

    The legal and regulatory challenges mobile wound care providers face, including HIPAA, OSHA, CMS, and payer requirements, with practical strategies for compliant documentation, accurate coding, and audit-ready records.

    Jeanine Maguire, PhD, MPT, CWSPAWSIC President · Moderator/Presenter
    Julie Rhodovi, MBA, CWCA, LBBHPAWSIC Treasurer · Co-Presenter
    Add to Google Calendar →
  2. 11:00 AM Ends 12:00 PM Room: Octavius 11

    Panel: Top 10 Best Evidenced Practices in Wound Care

    An expert panel on the intersection of nutrition, wound risk, and wound healing, and the evidence-based practices with the greatest impact on outcomes across care settings.

    Cassandre Voltaire, DO, ABWMSPAWSIC Board Member · Panelist
    Add to Google Calendar →

Sunday, October 18

  1. 10:30 AM Ends 11:30 AM Room: Octavius 4

    Putting Motion in Wound Care: PT Interventions in Wound Care

    How physical therapy interventions support wound healing, with evidence-based strategies to enhance tissue perfusion and integrate movement into comprehensive wound care plans.

    Stephanie Woelfel, PT, DPT, CWSPAWSIC Board Member · Co-Presenter
    Add to Google Calendar →

Session details come from the official SAWC Fall 2026 agenda and are subject to change. Confirm times and rooms on site.

Going to SAWC Fall? Find us there.

PAWSIC is exhibiting all four days. Stop by to meet the team, ask about membership, and connect with clinicians working in post-acute wound care.
