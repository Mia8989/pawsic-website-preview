Featured Event September 9, 2026 | 3:00 PM EST · Online Webinar

Transitions of Wound Care: Ensuring Safe Continuity of Care Across the Continuum

Wound care rarely stays in one place. This PAWSIC webinar examines the communication, clinical, and operational gaps that appear when patients move across the continuum, from acute to outpatient, SNF/LTC, and home health, and shares standardized workflows plus the PAWSIC Universal Wound Transition Checklist to help teams preserve continuity of care.

Register Free →

What You Will Learn

Identify the primary communication, clinical, and operational gaps during patient handoffs that elevate the risk of wound decline, infection, and re-hospitalization.
Analyze the operational challenges and clinical priorities of the different care settings across the continuum: acute, outpatient, SNF/LTC, and home health.
Implement standardized, actionable workflow recommendations designed to streamline the transition process and preserve clinical continuity.
Use the PAWSIC Universal Wound Transition Checklist as a practical, day-to-day resource to improve interdisciplinary collaboration and patient-centered discharge planning.
Faculty Jenn Bistrack, MPH, MS, RN Jennifer Bierhup, RN, WCC, CCM, IRNPA, FACCWS Mary Martin, BSN, RN, CWS
