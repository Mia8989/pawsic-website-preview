Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the PAWSIC Mobile Wound Group Checklist?

The PAWSIC Mobile Wound Group (MWG) Checklist 2026 is a standardized framework for evaluating, aligning, and optimizing mobile wound care practices across post-acute care settings. It covers competencies for both employed wound providers and contracted wound provider groups.

Who is the checklist for?

It supports three groups: wound providers and wound provider groups building or improving a program, healthcare leaders at any post-acute site of service who are selecting a wound provider or group, and patients or responsible parties who want a reference guide when choosing one.

Which sections do I complete?

Complete Section I, Core Standards, in full. Then complete only the setting-specific add-on for your care setting: Skilled Nursing Facility (SNF), Home Health Agency (HHA), or Assisted Living / Independent Living (ALF/IL).

How is the checklist scored?

Each criterion is scored from 0 to 3: 0 is Absent, 1 is Emerging, 2 is Established, and 3 is Best-in-Class. Critical items are flagged with a star to signal elevated regulatory or clinical risk that needs priority follow-up.

Can I type directly into the PDF?

Yes. All four PDFs are fillable, so you can type scores, comments, and evidence notes on screen and save your copy, or print them and complete them by hand.

Is the checklist free?

Yes. The checklist is free with a free PAWSIC membership. Signing up takes about a minute, needs no credit card, and opens the download page right away.

Does the checklist replace our policies or regulations?

No. The checklist is solely a guide. It is not intended to replace your facility or organization's policies and procedures or any local, state, or federal requirements.

Questions? We're here.

For questions about the checklist or how to use it with your team, reach PAWSIC at info@pawsic.org.
