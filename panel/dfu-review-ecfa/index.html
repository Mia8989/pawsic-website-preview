Introduction

Why these statements matter

Residents living in long-term care (LTC) facilities represent a highly vulnerable population facing compounded physiological risks from advanced age, peripheral neuropathy, vascular disease, and multiple chronic comorbidities that significantly delay wound healing.

To bridge the persistent evidence-to-practice gap and standardize frontline clinical workflows, these evidence-based practice statements have been synthesized from the most recent clinical trials and global consensus guidelines. The primary aim is to optimize and better manage diabetic foot ulcer (DFU) care and mitigate lower-extremity complications within the post-acute and long-term care continuum.

Recognizing that effective wound management requires a collaborative approach, these statements are designed to serve as a practical clinical guide for an interdisciplinary team, including physicians, wound care specialists, physical therapists, dietitians, nursing leaders, and frontline care staff.
